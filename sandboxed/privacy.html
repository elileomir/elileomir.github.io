Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers "Sandboxed Uploader", a private application used only by the owner of the Sandboxed YouTube channel (@sandboxedlab). It is not available to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to:

  • upload videos and set their details (title, description, thumbnail) on the Sandboxed channel;
  • read the Sandboxed channel's own information and analytics;
  • read and reply to comments on the Sandboxed channel's own videos.

It only acts on the Sandboxed channel. It does not access anyone else's account, contacts, email or files.

What we store

The app stores the sign-in token and the channel's own analytics on servers controlled by the channel owner, so it can publish and report on schedule. Public comments on our videos are processed only to read and answer them, and are not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data from this app with third parties. Data is sent only to Google (YouTube) to perform the actions above.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, we delete stored data within 30 days.
